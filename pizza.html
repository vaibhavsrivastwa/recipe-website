Pizza

Ingredients

  • Pizza dough
  • Tomato sauce
  • Mozzarella cheese
  • Toppings: vegetables, pepperoni, chicken

Instructions

  1. Spread tomato sauce on rolled dough.
  2. Add cheese and toppings.
  3. Bake at 220°C for 15 minutes.

Preparation Time: 30 minutes

Difficulty: Easy
