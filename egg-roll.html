Kolkata Egg Roll

Ingredients

  • Paratha (flatbread)
  • 2 eggs
  • Onions, cucumber, green chili
  • Tomato ketchup

Instructions

  1. Cook paratha on a pan.
  2. Whisk eggs, pour on pan, place paratha over it.
  3. Fill with onions, cucumber, chili, and ketchup. Roll and serve.

Preparation Time: 20 minutes

Difficulty: Easy
