Biryani

Ingredients

  • 2 cups basmati rice
  • 500g chicken
  • 2 onions, 2 tomatoes
  • Spices: cumin, coriander, garam masala
  • Yogurt, ginger, garlic

Instructions

  1. Soak rice for 30 minutes.
  2. Cook chicken with spices, yogurt, onions, and tomatoes.
  3. Layer rice and chicken, steam until fragrant.

Preparation Time: 60 minutes

Difficulty: Medium
