Tacos

Ingredients

  • 8 small tortillas
  • 250g minced beef or chicken
  • Onions, tomatoes, lettuce
  • Cheddar cheese
  • Salsa or hot sauce
  • Spices: chili powder, cumin, paprika

Instructions

  1. Cook minced meat with spices until browned.
  2. Warm tortillas on a pan.
  3. Fill tortillas with meat, vegetables, cheese, and salsa.
  4. Fold and serve immediately.

Preparation Time: 25 minutes

Difficulty: Easy
