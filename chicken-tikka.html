Chicken Tikka

Ingredients

  • 500g boneless chicken
  • Yogurt, lemon juice
  • Spices: chili powder, turmeric, garam masala
  • Ginger-garlic paste

Instructions

  1. Marinate chicken with yogurt, spices, and lemon juice for 2 hours.
  2. Skewer and grill until cooked.
  3. Serve hot with chutney.

Preparation Time: 45 minutes

Difficulty: Easy
